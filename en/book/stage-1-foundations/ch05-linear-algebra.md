# Ch 5 · Linear Algebra: Geometric Intuition

> **Stage**: 第一阶 · Foundations (L5 → L4) | **Pillar**: Math Foundations

## Core Question

What is a matrix? Why is it everywhere?

## Soul Insight

A matrix is a spatial transformation, not a table of numbers. A vector is a direction and magnitude, not a list of coordinates. Once you see linear algebra geometrically, matrix multiplication stops being a mechanical procedure and becomes "apply transformation B, then transformation A."

## Concepts

- Vectors: geometric interpretation, dot product, projection
- Matrix as transformation: rotation, scaling, shear
- Matrix multiplication as composition of transformations
- Eigenvalues/eigenvectors: directions a transformation only stretches, not rotates
- Linear independence and basis

## Practice Anchor

Two concrete examples where the geometric view clarifies everything:

1. **Image transformation**: rotation matrix applied to pixels — why does the formula work?
2. **Recommendation systems**: matrix factorization (UV decomposition) as finding latent factors — why do low-rank approximations work?
